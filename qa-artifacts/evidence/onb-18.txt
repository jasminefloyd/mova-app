AI REVENUE CENTER
Cancellations happen. Mova fills the chair.

When a client cancels, Mova finds the regulars who are due, drafts a text and sends it in small waves once you approve. First to book gets the spot. Everyone else gets a polite "just taken."

Approve each text, or let rules you set do it
Rebooking reminders that stop when a client books or opts out

AI CONCIERGE
Answers while you're behind the chair.

Your assistant replies to texts, missed calls and your booking page, in English or Spanish. It books open times at your suite, sends requests for salon times and always says it's AI. Quotes, complaints and anything unusual come straight to you.
