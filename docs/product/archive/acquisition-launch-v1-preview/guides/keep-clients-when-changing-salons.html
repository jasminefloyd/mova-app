How to Keep Your Clients When Changing Salons

Changing salons should not mean rebuilding your career from zero. Here is a practical, respectful transition plan.

Start with your agreement

Review any relevant salon agreement, non-solicitation obligations, and local rules before contacting clients. Do not export salon-owned data without authorization.

Build a professional identity clients recognize

Maintain a public portfolio, clear service descriptions, and an easy-to-share booking link that you control.

Communicate transparently

Tell clients where you will be working and how they can book, using only channels and contact permissions you are authorized to use.

Make the transition easy

Publish your new availability, directions, service list, and booking policies. Where appointments require salon approval, make that clear.

Keep relationships warm

Ask clients for consent to receive updates and rebooking reminders. Focus on helpful follow-ups, not repeated unsolicited messages.
