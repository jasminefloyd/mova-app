Booth Rental vs Commission for Hairstylists

The better arrangement depends on your client demand, expenses, autonomy, and the support a salon provides.

How commission works

A salon retains an agreed percentage of service revenue. Confirm who pays for supplies, marketing, processing fees, and cancellations.

How booth rental works

You typically pay fixed rent for space. Ask what is included and whether rent is due when you have few appointments.

Compare the full economics

Model realistic revenue, rent, commission, supplies, insurance, software, and taxes. Also value salon-provided leads and operational support.

Consider the nonfinancial tradeoffs

Evaluate scheduling control, client ownership, location flexibility, cancellation policies, and access to equipment.
