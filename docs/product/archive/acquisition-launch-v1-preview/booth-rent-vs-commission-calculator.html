Booth rent or commission: which leaves you with more?

Estimate your weekly earnings under both arrangements. Change the numbers to reflect your actual offers—not industry averages.

How to interpret this estimate

The calculator assumes the same service revenue and shared expenses in both models. Commission earnings equal revenue multiplied by your retained percentage, less expenses. Booth-rental earnings equal revenue less rent and expenses. Actual take-home may also depend on supplies, taxes, tips, card fees, insurance, salon-provided leads, and your agreement.

Important: This is a planning estimate, not tax, legal, or financial advice.
