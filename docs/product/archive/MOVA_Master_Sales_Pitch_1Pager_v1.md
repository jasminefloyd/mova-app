# MOVA — Master Sales Pitch | One-Page Enablement Sheet
**Version 1.0 · 2026-11-04 · Internal sales enablement · Product is in development; do not imply general availability.**

## The pitch (20 seconds)
**“Your chair may move. Your business shouldn't.”** Mova is the mobile-first business home being built for independent hairstylists. It brings your public portfolio, booking link, work locations, client relationships, payment visibility, and rebooking opportunities together—so your professional identity travels with you. **Your business moves with you.**

## Who to talk to / what to listen for
**Best fit:** US booth/chair renters, commission stylists who control their own clientele, multi-location stylists, and professionals preparing to change salons. **Listen for:** “My clients book through the salon,” “I work in two places,” “I lose track of rebooking,” “My portfolio is on Instagram,” “I don't know which payments came through the salon.” **Not ideal:** chain-salon buyers seeking payroll, staff POS or full salon ERP.

## Discovery questions
1. Where do new clients find your work and book you today?
2. What changes when you switch salons or work at another location?
3. Who controls your calendar and confirms appointments?
4. How do you track repeat visits, deposits, and salon-collected payments?
5. Would a free professional showcase be useful before you change booking tools?

## What makes Mova different
**Portable artist identity + multi-location truth + hair-first portfolio + transparent payment collection + consent-based rebooking.** Unlike a generic salon ERP, Mova starts with the individual professional. **Do not claim other products lack these capabilities** without a current verified comparison.

## Common objections (short answers)
- **“I already use booking software.”** “You may keep it while starting with a free showcase. Mova's value is portable identity and multi-location-aware operations; we won't promise universal integrations.”
- **“My salon controls appointments.”** “Mova will use a request-to-book flow when the salon system must be checked; it won't falsely confirm an opening.”
- **“Will this double-book me?”** “Mova prevents conflicts in its own inventory, but V1 does not automatically block every external salon or Apple/Google calendar event.”
- **“Do I have to use Stripe?”** “Not to publish your showcase or track salon-collected services; online payments depend on eligible onboarding.”
- **“Will AI message my clients?”** “Not without your approval and the required consent.”
- **“Can my Spanish-speaking clients book?”** “English and Spanish are planned for V1 booking and core communications.”
